# Lumora Features

## 1. Core Player Features

### Audio
- **Gapless playback** – Seamless transition between tracks with no silence.
- **Crossfade** – Smoothly blend the end of one track into the start of the next.
- **ReplayGain / volume normalization** – Consistent volume across all tracks.
- **Sleep timer** – Auto-stop playback after a set duration.
- **Playback speed (0.5x–3x)** – Slow down or speed up audio.
- **Pitch correction** – Adjust pitch independently of speed.
- **Audio balance (L/R)** – Shift audio between left and right channels.
- **Loudness enhancer** – Boost perceived loudness.
- **Bass boost** – Emphasize low-end frequencies.
- **10–32 band equalizer** – Fine-grained frequency control.

### Video
- **Picture-in-Picture** – Mini floating player while using other apps.
- **Gesture controls** – Swipe for brightness, volume, and seeking.
- **Subtitle support** – SRT, ASS, and VTT formats.
- **Subtitle downloading** – Fetch subtitles from online sources.
- **Playback speed** – Variable speed for video content.
- **Frame-by-frame stepping** – Advance one frame at a time.

## 2. Library Management

### Smart Scanning
- **Incremental rescans** – Only scan for new/changed files.
- **Background scanning** – Scan without blocking the UI.
- **Duplicate detection** – Identify and handle duplicate files.
- **Missing file cleanup** – Remove entries for deleted files.

### Organization
- Albums, Artists, Genres, Folders, Years, Recently Added, Most Played.

### Advanced Sorting
- Sort by: Name, Date Added, Duration, Size, Play Count, Last Played.

### Tag Editing
- Edit: Title, Artist, Album, Genre, Year, Track Number — no external software needed.

---

## 3. Smart Playlists

### Automatic Playlists
- Recently Added, Recently Played, Favorites, Most Played, Never Played.

### Rules-Based Playlists
- Define rules (e.g., `Genre = Rock AND Year > 2015 AND Play Count < 10`) and auto-generate playlists.
